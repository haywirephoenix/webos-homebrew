Runs Tailscale on the TV, so you can reach it, and reach your other devices from it, over your tailnet. Not affiliated with Tailscale Inc.

The app installs the official Tailscale build and keeps it updated, signs the TV in with a QR code, connects and disconnects, and picks an exit node. It lists the devices on your tailnet and shows what Tailscale is doing in a console.

Turn on Boot and Tailscale starts and connects each time the TV starts. It needs root, and does its work through the Homebrew Channel.

Looking for testers! Built on an LG BX with webOS 5.4. If you try it on another TV, please tell us how it went in an issue on GitHub.
